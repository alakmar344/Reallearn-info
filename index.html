Active recall

You can't skip ahead until you understand

This is the heart of active learning. After each part, a short quiz slides up — and the next part stays locked until you've truly grasped what came before.

  • 2 questions, 4 options each, after every part
  • Score 100% to unlock the next part — no skipping, no skimming
  • Every answer reveals an exhaustive, mini-lesson explanation
  • Wrong answers shake; correct answers pulse — feedback is immediate and tactile
  • It's not a test that judges you — it's a gate that protects your understanding

Questions

Frequently asked

How is this different from a search engine or chatbot? +

Instead of one paragraph or a wall of links, ReaLearn builds a structured three-part journey — Foundation, Mechanism, and Real World Now — with quizzes you must pass to progress. It's teaching on demand, not a one-shot answer.

Do I really have to pass a quiz to continue? +

Yes — and that's the point. Each part ends with a short quiz, and you need a perfect score to unlock the next part. It's a comprehension gate that protects your understanding, with deep explanations for every answer.

Which languages are supported? +

English, Hindi, Gujarati, Tamil, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, and Kannada — and lessons are generated natively in your chosen language, never machine-translated afterward.

Can I choose how difficult my lessons are? +

Absolutely. Pick from Class 6–8, Class 9–10, or College / Advanced, and the entire lesson — vocabulary, examples, and rigor — adapts to match.

Is my data private? +

Yes. Sessions are authenticated, content is moderated, and you can export all your data as JSON or permanently delete your account at any time. Consent is transparent and versioned.

How long does a typical lesson take? +

Most learners complete a full three-part journey in 10–15 minutes. The reading timer encourages you to absorb each part fully before the quiz unlocks, so you're never rushed.

Can I revisit completed lessons? +

Yes — every completed journey is saved to your personal library. You can reopen, review, or retake any past lesson at any time. The library also de-duplicates intelligently so it stays clean.

What subjects does ReaLearn cover? +

ReaLearn auto-classifies across 11 disciplines: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, Economics, History, Geography, Computer Science, Literature, Philosophy, and General Knowledge. You can ask about any topic within these domains.
